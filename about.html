ABOUT THE FOUNDATION

A heart for people. A commitment to service.

We exist to serve people with compassion, dignity and practical action while building partnerships that create lasting hope.

WHO WE ARE
Serving people, one relationship at a time.

A Passion For God's Glory Foundation is a people-centered organization committed to walking alongside children, mothers, young people and communities. We believe meaningful change begins by listening, caring and responding to real needs.

Our Christian foundation shapes our values and service, while our doors remain welcoming to people from every background.

Our Mission

Bring hope, restore dignity and serve with compassion through practical support, relationships and partnerships.

Our Vision

Stronger communities where people are respected, supported and able to flourish.

OUR VALUES
How we want to serve.

Compassion

We listen, care and treat people with kindness.

Integrity

We aim to be honest, responsible and accountable.

Partnership

We work with communities and supporters rather than working over them.

OUR PROGRAM PROPOSALS
Small acts of care can open the door to lasting hope.

These proposals describe the programmes we hope to develop with communities and responsible partners. Each initiative will be shaped by local needs, available resources and the voices of the people it is intended to serve. We welcome donors and partners who want to help turn thoughtful plans into practical, measurable support.

PROPOSAL 01

Children’s Care, Joy & Hope

Every child deserves to feel seen, safe and encouraged. We propose community visits and child-focused activities that offer companionship, positive encouragement and connections to essential support where needs are identified.

Planned activities

  • Spend caring, meaningful time with children through play, conversation and encouragement.
  • Identify needs respectfully with caregivers and trusted community contacts.
  • Connect families with suitable services or practical assistance when available.

What support could make possible

Safe activity materials, essential supplies, local transport and well-planned community visits. Children’s privacy and safeguarding will remain central.

PROPOSAL 02

Care for Mothers & Women

Pregnancy and difficult seasons can bring worry, isolation and extra needs. This proposal aims to help pregnant mothers and vulnerable women feel supported, respected and better connected to appropriate care.

Planned activities

  • Listen to women and understand the support they say they need.
  • Provide practical essentials when resources allow.
  • Share reliable information and refer people to qualified health and community services.

What support could make possible

Mother-and-baby essentials, transport for referrals, community outreach and partnerships with qualified service providers. The programme would complement—not replace—professional medical care.

PROPOSAL 03

Youth Skills & Confidence

Young people need encouragement, useful skills and people who believe in their potential. We propose supportive sessions that help youth build confidence, make positive choices and explore learning and work opportunities.

Planned activities

  • Hold group conversations on life skills, responsibility and personal goals.
  • Invite experienced volunteers and mentors to share practical knowledge.
  • Help participants discover relevant education, training and employment pathways.

What support could make possible

Learning materials, training sessions, mentor engagement and links to local skills programmes. Activities will be developed around the needs and interests of young people.

PROPOSAL 04

Community Visits & Compassion

Some people carry difficult burdens quietly. This proposal focuses on visiting and listening to people who may feel forgotten, offering respectful companionship and helping connect them with available support.

Planned activities

  • Arrange respectful home and community visits with consent.
  • Listen without judgement and record needs only when appropriate and safe.
  • Follow up where possible and refer urgent or specialist needs to suitable services.

What support could make possible

Local transport, essential items, volunteer preparation and responsible follow-up. Personal stories and photographs will only be shared with appropriate informed consent.

PROPOSAL 05

Community Partnerships & Outreach

Lasting change is stronger when communities, individual donors, businesses, faith groups and other organisations work together. We propose building partnerships that respond to locally identified needs with care and accountability.

Planned activities

  • Listen to community representatives and agree on clear priorities.
  • Develop focused outreach activities with suitable partners.
  • Share progress updates, lessons learned and responsible use of donated resources.

What support could make possible

Partner-led activities, shared resources, volunteer training and community outreach. Each partnership will have agreed responsibilities and a clear purpose.
