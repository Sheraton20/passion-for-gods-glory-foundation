Who we are

Rooted in faith. Grounded in people.

A Passion For God's Glory Foundation exists to serve people with compassion, dignity and practical action. Our Christian faith inspires our service, while our welcome remains open to people of every faith and background.

See our workConnect with us
Why we exist

People should not have to face difficult seasons alone.

We believe meaningful service begins by seeing people, listening carefully and responding to real needs. That can mean encouragement for a child, support for a mother, opportunity for a young person, practical help for a family, or a relationship that restores hope.

OUR APPROACH

Listen → Walk together → Serve with care

We aim to work with communities rather than simply do things to communities. As the foundation grows, we will build local partnerships, document our work and publish appropriate information about our impact and governance.

What guides us

Simple values. Serious responsibility.

01DignityPeople are more than problems to solve. We treat every person with respect.
02CompassionWe want our service to be personal, kind and practical.
03IntegrityWe will be honest about what we have done, what we have learned and what we have not yet achieved.
Our vision

A world where hope is practical.

We are building toward a foundation that can serve across Africa and, over time, alongside communities and partners around the world. We want service to be measured not only by what is given, but by whether people are respected, strengthened and able to move forward.
