ABOUT THE FOUNDATION

A heart for people. A commitment to service.

We exist to serve people with compassion, dignity and practical action while building partnerships that create lasting hope.

WHO WE ARE
Serving people, one relationship at a time.

A Passion For God's Glory Foundation is a people-centered organization committed to walking alongside children, mothers, young people and communities. We believe meaningful change begins by listening, caring and responding to real needs.

Our Christian foundation shapes our values and service, while our doors remain welcoming to people from every background.

Our Mission

Bring hope, restore dignity and serve with compassion through practical support, relationships and partnerships.

Our Vision

Stronger communities where people are respected, supported and able to flourish.

OUR VALUES
How we want to serve.

Compassion

We listen, care and treat people with kindness.

Integrity

We aim to be honest, responsible and accountable.

Partnership

We work with communities and supporters rather than working over them.

OUR PUBLIC PROMISE
Compassion that respects people. Accountability that respects every contribution.

Our promise is to listen before acting, protect the dignity and privacy of the people we serve, use resources responsibly, and communicate honestly about what we can—and cannot—deliver. We welcome people of every faith and background, and practical support will never depend on religious participation, photographs or public testimony.

We will speak with hope, plan with care, and report with honesty.

We are building our programmes with community input and trusted partners. The proposals are planning documents, not claims that all activities are already funded or operational.

OUR PROPOSED COMMUNITY PROGRAMMES
No one should have to face hardship alone.

Behind every need is a human life: a mother trying to protect her baby, a child who wants to learn but cannot afford school, a young person searching for a chance, or a family wondering where they will sleep tonight. These are not just problems to list on a page. They are people deserving of safety, dignity, care and hope.

A Passion For God’s Glory Foundation wants to work alongside communities to respond with practical compassion. Our Christian faith inspires us to love and serve; our invitation is open to people of every faith and background. The programmes below are proposals we hope to develop with community members, donors and qualified partners. They are not claims that services or funding are already in place.

01
CHILDREN • EDUCATION • PROTECTION

Let Every Child Have a Chance

A child should be thinking about lessons, friendships and dreams—not whether poverty will keep them outside the classroom. When school costs, lack of supplies, family hardship or unstable living conditions interrupt education, a child’s future can feel smaller before it has even begun.

We want to help children take steps towards learning, safety and belonging. We propose working with caregivers, schools and community partners to understand what is keeping children out of school and what support may help them return and remain there.

What we hope to do

  • Identify school-related barriers with families and, where appropriate, local schools.
  • Help provide learning materials, uniforms or school-related assistance when funds and verified needs allow.
  • Create safe opportunities for reading, play, mentoring and encouragement.
  • Refer child-protection concerns to appropriate services and keep children’s information confidential.

Intended impact

More children supported to enrol in or continue education, improved access to basic learning materials, and children who feel encouraged and included. We will track assistance provided and follow up where possible rather than promise outcomes we cannot yet guarantee.

02
PREGNANT MOTHERS • WOMEN • NEWBORNS

A Mother Should Not Have to Struggle Alone

Pregnancy can be a season of hope, but poverty, loneliness and uncertainty can make it frightening. Some mothers are trying to prepare for a baby while worrying about food, transport to care, a safe place to stay or the essentials a newborn will need. No mother should feel that asking for help makes her less worthy.

We want to stand beside mothers with kindness rather than judgement. This proposal would connect women with practical support and trusted services, while respecting their choices, privacy and dignity.

What we hope to do

  • Listen to pregnant mothers and vulnerable women to understand their most urgent needs.
  • Help with essential mother-and-baby supplies when resources allow.
  • Support access to antenatal and postnatal services through referrals and, where budgeted, transport assistance.
  • Connect mothers with community support and reliable information from qualified providers.

Intended impact

Mothers better connected to care and community support, families receiving essential items where available, and fewer women feeling that they must face difficult circumstances alone. Medical care will always be referred to qualified health professionals.

03
SHELTER • BASIC NEEDS • FAMILY DIGNITY

A Safe Place to Rest, A Chance to Begin Again

A home is more than a roof. It is the place where a child can sleep without fear, a parent can gather strength, and a family can begin to rebuild. When people face unsafe housing, displacement or the threat of sleeping outside, even ordinary daily tasks can become overwhelming.

We hope to develop a community shelter and essential-needs response in partnership with trusted local organisations. Until resources and partnerships are secured, our role will be to assess needs, make appropriate referrals and offer limited practical help where possible—not to imply that we already operate a shelter.

What we hope to do

  • Identify families at risk and understand immediate safety concerns respectfully.
  • Connect people with existing shelter, social welfare and community services where available.
  • Provide essential items or short-term practical assistance when approved funding permits.
  • Explore sustainable shelter partnerships with clear safeguarding, eligibility and operating plans.

Intended impact

Families better connected to safe accommodation options, urgent needs identified earlier, and practical support delivered transparently. Any future shelter project would proceed only after a feasible budget, qualified partners and safeguarding procedures are in place.

04
YOUTH • SKILLS • LIVELIHOODS

Give Young People a Fair Chance

It is painful to watch a young person willing to work but unable to find an opportunity. Without guidance, skills or a path to earn a living, hope can slowly turn into discouragement. Young people are not a lost generation; they are people with gifts that need room to grow.

We propose practical learning and mentoring that helps young people build confidence, develop useful skills and connect with employment, apprenticeships or responsible small-business opportunities. We will not promise jobs that we cannot guarantee; we want to help create pathways and partnerships that make opportunities more reachable.

What we hope to do

  • Consult youth about the skills and work opportunities that are most relevant locally.
  • Organise life-skills, job-readiness, digital-literacy and financial-literacy sessions.
  • Seek mentors, trainers and employers willing to offer guidance or practical placements.
  • Explore small livelihood projects only after proper training, costing and accountability planning.

Intended impact

Young people gaining practical knowledge, stronger job-readiness and connections to mentors or training. We aim to record participation, completed training and referrals to opportunities, and follow up on outcomes where participants consent.

05
HIV AWARENESS • HEALTH • DIGNITY

Care Without Stigma. Hope Without Judgement.

People affected by HIV deserve accurate information, respectful care and the freedom to live without shame. Fear, misinformation, stigma and financial hardship can make it harder for people to seek support or stay connected to services. No one should be reduced to a diagnosis or made to feel less loved.

We hope to partner with qualified health organisations to share reliable HIV-prevention information, encourage voluntary testing through appropriate providers, support referrals and help reduce stigma. We will protect confidentiality and never disclose anyone’s HIV status without informed permission.

What we hope to do

  • Support community education using accurate information from recognised health providers.
  • Connect people to qualified testing, treatment and counselling services.
  • Offer respectful practical support or transport assistance if funded and appropriate.
  • Include families and youth in age-appropriate health education without blame or discrimination.

Intended impact

Improved access to trustworthy information and referrals, stronger community understanding, and a more welcoming environment for people affected by HIV. The foundation will not provide medical diagnosis or treatment unless through properly qualified partners.

06
COMMUNITY CARE • FAITH • HOPE

Let No One Feel Forgotten

Sometimes the first help a person needs is someone who will sit beside them, listen without rushing, and remind them that their life matters. A visit, a shared conversation, a meal or a sincere word of encouragement cannot solve every hardship—but it can be the beginning of a bridge towards help.

Our faith calls us to serve with love, humility and hope. We want to make space for prayer and spiritual encouragement for those who welcome it, while respecting each person’s beliefs and never making practical help conditional on religious participation.

What we hope to do

  • Arrange respectful visits with consent to children, families and people experiencing isolation or hardship.
  • Listen to community members and connect them with suitable services and support.
  • Invite trained volunteers and local partners to offer safe, reliable follow-up.
  • Provide optional prayer or spiritual encouragement only when welcomed by the person.

Intended impact

People feeling heard and treated with dignity, needs being identified earlier, and more connections to appropriate practical and community support. We will seek feedback and document activities without exposing private stories.

HOW WE WILL MEASURE IMPACT

Honest progress, not empty promises.

As programmes begin, we aim to report activities delivered, people reached in privacy-protecting aggregate form, support provided, referrals made, training completed and lessons learned. We will distinguish planned targets from actual results and explain when a target changes.

HOW WE WILL PROTECT DIGNITY

People are never just a fundraising story.

We will seek informed consent before sharing identifiable stories or images, protect children and health information, and avoid making support conditional on public testimony, photographs or religious participation. Donors can ask for project updates and a clear account of the use of funds.

BE PART OF THE HOPE

One caring decision can help open a door.

A donation, a professional skill, a school partnership, mother-and-baby supplies, a training opportunity or a trusted community connection can help us move from a proposal to a carefully planned response. We invite individuals, businesses, community groups, churches and international partners to speak with us about what is possible.

We do not ask you to believe in promises without evidence. We ask you to join us in building a compassionate plan, costing it responsibly, serving people with dignity and reporting honestly on the work.
