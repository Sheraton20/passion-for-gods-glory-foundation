VOLUNTEER COORDINATION

Volunteer Placement Agreement

Please review the placement terms carefully. This agreement records the volunteer's understanding of their responsibilities and the foundation's self-funded placement model.

Important: A placement is not confirmed by completing this form alone. The foundation must review the application, confirm the role and supervisor, complete applicable checks, and verify any required permissions before service begins. This template should be reviewed for compliance with Ugandan law before formal organizational adoption.

1. Nature of the placement

The volunteer offers time and skills voluntarily to support approved programs of A Passion for God's Glory Foundation. The placement is unpaid unless a separate written agreement, consistent with applicable law, states otherwise. The volunteer title does not override any legal employment rights that may apply to the actual working arrangement.

2. Expenses and self-funded participation

Unless a specific written arrangement is approved in advance, the volunteer is responsible for personal expenses, including transport, meals, accommodation, travel, insurance, visas or permits where applicable, and personal medical costs. The foundation does not promise salary, stipend, sponsorship, reimbursement, accommodation, meals, or travel funding. No expense should be incurred expecting reimbursement without prior written approval.

3. Foundation support

Subject to capacity and available resources, the foundation will provide an appropriate placement description, a designated supervisor or contact, relevant orientation, reasonable guidance, and information about applicable procedures. The foundation may postpone or decline a placement where resources, safeguarding, suitability, or legal permissions are not in place.

4. Volunteer conduct

  • Follow lawful instructions, safety guidance, and the foundation's code of conduct.
  • Treat colleagues, community members, children, and beneficiaries with dignity and respect.
  • Protect confidential information and personal data.
  • Do not make financial commitments, collect donations, promise assistance, or speak officially for the foundation without written authorization.
  • Report safety concerns, suspected abuse, misconduct, or safeguarding incidents promptly.

5. Safeguarding and media

The volunteer must follow safeguarding procedures, required screening, and supervision requirements. No inappropriate contact, exploitation, harassment, or abuse is permitted. Photographs, videos, interviews, or personal stories involving beneficiaries may only be collected or shared with proper authorization and informed consent. Access to beneficiaries may be restricted until all required checks and training are complete.

6. International placements

International volunteers are responsible for ensuring they have the required travel documents, insurance, and immigration permissions. A foundation invitation or placement letter does not guarantee a visa, permit, sponsorship, accommodation, or permission to start activities. Activities requiring prior authorization must not begin until approvals are in place.

7. Duration, concerns, and ending the placement

The placement dates and duties must be confirmed by the foundation separately. Either party may request to end the placement; reasonable notice is encouraged when practical. The foundation may suspend or end a placement where safety, program capacity, legal compliance, or serious policy violations require it. Volunteers can raise concerns with their supervisor or another designated contact.

8. Acknowledgement and acceptance

By submitting the form below, I confirm that I have read and understood these terms, that the information I provide is accurate to the best of my knowledge, and that I understand my placement is not confirmed until the foundation issues confirmation. I understand that this online acknowledgement is a record of acceptance and may not replace any additional signature or document required by law or the foundation.
