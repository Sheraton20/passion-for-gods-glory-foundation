THE HEART BEHIND THE STORE

A store with a reason to exist.

Some of the most meaningful changes begin with small acts of care: making time for a child, encouraging a young person, standing beside a mother, or showing a family that their community has not forgotten them.

A Passion for God’s Glory Foundation was created around that spirit of service. We want to build a practical, compassionate way to support people with dignity—not only through words, but through consistent action and relationships that matter.

The Good Things Store is one part of that journey. It brings useful, giftable products together with a bigger purpose. Rather than asking people only to give, it gives them another way to participate: choose something they value, share the story, and help create a more sustainable foundation for our community work.

We want this store to welcome everyone who believes that kindness, dignity and opportunity matter. You do not need to share the same background or beliefs to be part of something good.

“A thoughtful purchase can carry a purpose far beyond the thing itself.”
