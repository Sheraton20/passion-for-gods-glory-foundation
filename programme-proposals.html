DELIVERY & ACCOUNTABILITY

How we will account for the work

Before launch, each programme should have a named responsible lead, documented eligibility criteria, partner checks, safeguarding steps, an approved line-item budget and a clear way to receive feedback or complaints.

WhenMinimum public reporting commitment
Before launchPublish final approved budget, target, dates, eligibility and delivery partners (where confirmed).
Monthly during deliveryInternally reconcile receipts, payments, approvals, activities and funds remaining.
QuarterlyPublish a short update: funds received, expenditure to date, outputs, risks and changes from plan.
At closePublish final expenditure, outputs against targets, lessons, remaining funds and any agreed next steps.

Donor restrictions, privacy requirements and local legal obligations must be respected. Public reports should use aggregate information and should never expose children's identities or sensitive health details.
