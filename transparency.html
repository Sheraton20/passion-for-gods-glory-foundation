ACCOUNTABILITY IN ACTION

Trust is built by showing our work.

We believe every person deserves dignity and every contribution deserves responsible care. This page explains what we will report, how we will check it, and how supporters can follow progress without exposing the private lives of the people we serve.

See our reporting commitments
OUR CURRENT REPORTING POSITION

Honest about what is—and is not—verified.

We are establishing a consistent public reporting process. We will publish financial totals and programme results only after the relevant records have been checked. We will not present planned activities as completed work or treat draft budgets as money received.

Important status note: This page is a transparency framework, not a financial statement or an impact report. Verified reporting-period figures, receipts, expenditure, balances and confirmed programme outcomes have not yet been entered here. That is intentional: we will not invent numbers or imply that draft programme targets have already been achieved.
₦
Awaiting verified records

Financial reporting

Income, restricted donations, expenditure, remaining balances and supporting records will be reconciled before publication. All public totals will state the reporting period and currency.

◎
Baseline to be established

Programme progress

Each programme will report its target, activities completed, people reached, evidence source, challenges and next steps. Targets will be clearly separated from actual results.

♡
Safeguarding commitment

People before publicity

We will protect children and vulnerable people. Names, identifiable photos, medical details and personal stories will not be published without appropriate informed consent and safeguarding review.

OUR REPORTING CYCLE

A repeatable process, not just promises.

1. Record

Keep receipts, donation records, delivery logs and approved programme evidence.

2. Reconcile

Compare records, bank/mobile-money statements and restricted-fund balances.

3. Review

Check totals, safeguarding risks, indicators and explanations before publishing.

4. Publish

Share a dated summary, progress against targets, limitations and next steps.

Our intended cadence is internal monthly reconciliation, public quarterly summaries when records and activity support them, and a programme close-out report. We will label any delayed or incomplete report clearly rather than imply it is complete.

QUESTIONS & CORRECTIONS

Accountability includes listening.

If you need clarification about a proposal, donation allocation or published report, please contact the foundation. We welcome respectful questions and will correct material errors transparently when identified.
