ABOUT THE FOUNDATION
A heart for people. A commitment to service.

A Passion For God's Glory Foundation exists to serve people with love, compassion and practical action. Our approach is centered on listening to communities, respecting every person's dignity and building partnerships that turn care into meaningful support.

Our Christian foundation shapes our values and our service, while our doors remain welcoming to people from every background.

MissionBring hope, restore dignity and serve with compassion.
VisionStronger communities where people can flourish.
ValuesFaith • Integrity • Compassion • Excellence • Partnership
