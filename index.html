Who we are

Faith inspires us. Compassion moves us.

A Passion For God's Glory Foundation exists to turn compassion into practical action. Our Christian foundation shapes our service, while our doors remain open to people of every faith and background.

We believe every person deserves dignity, kindness, encouragement and an opportunity to hope again.
