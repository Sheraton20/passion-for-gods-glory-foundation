Who we are

Rooted in faith. Grounded in people.

A Passion For God's Glory Foundation is a growing African foundation built around a simple conviction: people should not have to face difficult seasons alone.

Our Christian faith inspires the way we serve, while our work remains welcoming to people of every faith and background. We want every person we meet to feel seen, respected and valued.
